As part of a class project, we were tasked with taking apart a randomly assigned object to determine and describe how it works using our knowledge of fluid mechanics. My group was assigned an instant coffee maker, which is surprisingly complex and highly related to fluid mechanics.

My Contributions:
During the actual dissection of the coffee maker, I helped to explain a lot of the inner workings of the coffee maker to my colleagues. I also helped work on the presentation.
