This problem taught me many fundamental things about heat transfer. First of all, it taught me how to use the classic heat transfer equations, such as the equation for convective heat transfer, conductive heat transfer, and energy balance. It also taught me to think of things not just in terms of heat transfer, but relating heat transfer to electrical power, which is very applicable to industry and the real world. In the future, I believe these skills will be extremely valuable to the work I will be doing. I will also be able to build onto these skills by, for example, doing cost analysis comparing different insulation materials for a project.
