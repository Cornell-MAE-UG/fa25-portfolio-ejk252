Goal:
The goal of our project is to harness the power of solar energy to produce heat and electricity.

​Purpose:
An incentive for people in 3rd world countries to avoid using rudimentary cooking stoves, reducing CO2 emissions from cooking, preventing premature deaths from poor air quality

How It Works

​​The core of our project is the use of thermoelectric generators (TEGs). These are small chip-like devices which can generate electricity through the Seebeck effect. This is a phenomenon where a temperature difference between each side of a TEG causes a voltage to be generated and an electrical current to be produced by it. 

Spring 2025

During my first semester, I spent a lot of time familiarizing myself with the ins and outs of the project. I also used CAD and Ansys to test chassis modifications I made to the 3d model.


Fall 2025

This semester I am working on creating a greater temperature for the TEGs using more advanced insulation materials. Currently, we are attempting to fabricate Aerogel to further insulate the cool side of the TEG away from the copper plate.
